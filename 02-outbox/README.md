# 02-outbox — your outbox

**Where finished work leaves the studio.** When Tropo exports a deliverable — a Word doc, a PDF, a published piece — it lands here, ready to send.

Tropo's input is careful and its output is fast: `04-external-work/` is the on-ramp in, `02-outbox/` is the fast lane out, and your work lives permanently in `vault/`.
