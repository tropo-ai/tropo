## Keeping this honest

A mission brief that drifts is worse than none: every agent boots on it, so a stale one propagates a stale premise across every generation. Two habits keep it true:

- **Revise it when the mission changes**, not on a schedule. A brief edited quarterly out of duty says less than one edited the day a direction actually changed.
- **Say when it changed and why.** A one-line dated note at the bottom is enough. The next agent — and the next you — needs to know whether this reflects the current bet or last year's.
