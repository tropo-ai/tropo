# 04-external-work — bring your work into Tropo

**This folder is your import drop-zone. Put the files you already have here, and Tropo brings them into your studio — tracked, searchable, and linked — without changing your originals.**

This is the most important thing you can do on day one: get your real work *inside* the system, where your agents can see it and build on it.

---

## How to import

1. **Drop files or whole folders in here.** Word docs, PDFs, slide decks, spreadsheets, notes, a nested folder of mixed content — copy it all into `04-external-work/`.
2. **Ask your agent to bring it in** (or it happens the next time an agent starts up). Tropo walks this folder and governs what it finds.
3. **That's it.** Each file becomes a tracked entry in your Vault — with a stable ID, so you can search it, link to it, and reference it from projects and tasks.

Your original files are never modified. The source stays exactly as you dropped it; Tropo adds a governed record *alongside* it.

For text documents (like Word), Tropo can also create an editable markdown working-copy your agents can revise in place. Conversion for PDFs, slides, and spreadsheets is still growing — but every file you drop here is tracked and governed from the moment it lands, whatever its format.

---

## Where this fits

Tropo's input is meticulous and its output is fast — and the folders at your studio root tell you the shape at a glance:

- **`04-external-work/`** — import **in** (you are here): the careful on-ramp for bringing the outside world into the studio.
- **`02-outbox/`** — export **out**: finished work leaving the studio.
- **`vault/`** — where your work lives permanently once it's governed.

A few neighbors, so things land in the right place:

- Half-formed notes and quick captures you're *authoring* (not importing) → drop them in **`01-studio-inbox/`**.
- Outdated or cancelled work → **`99-recycle/`** (Tropo never hard-deletes; it recycles).
