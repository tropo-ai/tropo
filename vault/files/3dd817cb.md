Stage privately, fire once, verify the remote tag, main SHA, release object and uploaded
asset against `package_sha256`, then write the public receipt.

**The receipt is the commit point, and closure is a recoverable saga hung off it** —
not a close WorkflowNode (§AC8). One outward effect; retry is idempotent; a partial or
conflicting outcome stays OPEN with a journaled recovery path rather than being closed
on optimism.
