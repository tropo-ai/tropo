## Purpose

Run `sa.vault-integrity-auditor` once per week on the first Monday boot. Deep structural integrity checks — registry coherence, cross-reference resolution, governance contract auditing — beyond the daily health pass.

## Consent

**consent_mode: ask** (factory default). Does not fire before onboarding consent. After consent, owner may flip to `auto` to enable weekly in-session dispatch.

## Consequence

`consequence: medium` — the audit runner reads widely and emits crew events for structural issues. Verifier is the validator (d2b9c8e6) plus independent review of the audit report.
