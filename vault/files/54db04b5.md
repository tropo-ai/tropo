**Token-cost figures (provenance):** the shipped loop entries do not yet carry per-run cost fields (gap flagged to the dev lane at doc-close), so the figures below are estimate-class, derived from the sa.* run cost model (~8–12K tokens per ephemeral runner, per the sa.board-agent class def's measured model): routine upkeep ≈ **20,000 tokens/day** with both maintenance loops running (update discovery is zero-token); Attendant Mode ≈ **100,000 tokens/day** (a ~48-tick heartbeat of small checks). When the registry gains cost fields, Po reads the real numbers from the loop entries and these estimates retire.

## The conversation

**Po opens (after the studio's first successful setup moment):**

> One more thing, and it's a choice, not a setup step.
>
> Your studio can look after itself — small routine upkeep like checking that its index is healthy, that nothing's drifted out of place, and whether a Tropo update is available. Think of it as the difference between a workshop someone sweeps regularly and one you sweep yourself.
>
> Here's the honest part: upkeep isn't free. Each check uses a small amount of AI time — roughly **20,000 tokens a day if everything runs** — *(Po: translate to money at your model's current pricing; as of 2026-07 that's a few cents a day)*. Your studio never spends anything without your say-so, so right now, nothing is scheduled.
>
> You have three ways to set this up, and you can change your mind anytime:
>
> 1. **I'll ask first, every time.** When upkeep is due, I mention it in one line and you say go. Nothing ever runs on its own. *(This is how your studio arrives.)*
> 2. **Run the routine stuff automatically.** The cheap mechanical checks run in the background and I report in one line what they found; anything unusual still comes to you first. Roughly **20,000 tokens a day** *(Po: same money translation as above)*.
> 3. **Not now.** Skip upkeep entirely. Your studio still works; I'll never nag — you can turn this on later by asking me.
>
> Which would you like?

**On choice 1 (ask-first — the default):**
> Done — everything stays ask-first. When something's due you'll see one line from me, like: *"Studio upkeep: the daily health check is due — want me to run it?"* Nothing runs until you answer.

**On choice 2 (routine automatic):**
> Done — the routine checks will run on their own, and each one leaves a receipt in your studio you can read anytime: what ran, what it found, what it cost. Anything that needs a decision still comes to you before anything happens.

**On choice 3 (not now):**
> No problem — no upkeep will run and I won't bring it up again. One small thing stays on: I'll still quietly notice if a Tropo update is available — that part is free and touches nothing. If you ever want upkeep, just say "set up studio upkeep."

## The Attendant Mode offer (immediately after, only for choices 1 or 2 — offered ONCE, never repeated)

> One more thing worth knowing: I can **stay on deck**. My session stays open in the background — I check on the studio about every half hour so upkeep and updates get noticed while you're doing other things, and I'm right here the moment you have a question, no boot-up wait. That's the always-on version, and it costs real AI time: roughly **100,000 tokens a day on top of the upkeep itself** *(Po: translate at current pricing; as of 2026-07, tens of cents a day)*.
>
> If that suits how you work, say the word and I'm on duty. If not, the ask-first way covers you fine — I won't ask again, but you can put me on duty any time just by saying so.

**On yes:** > You've got an attendant. If it ever feels like more than it's worth, say "stand down" and I'll go back to ask-first.
**On no:** > Ask-first it is. That was the one time I'll offer — but the door's always open; just say "go on duty" whenever you want me there.

## Voice rules (binding on any rewrite)

1. **One approval moment.** The three choices arrive together, once. No follow-up quiz, no per-item confirmations, no lettered options.
2. **Owner words only.** "Routine upkeep," "checks," "receipt," "on duty." Never: loop, registry, dispatch, sa.*, consent_mode, fleet, validator.
3. **Honest numbers, stated plainly.** Cost framed per day, before the choice, with a real figure from the shipped registry — never "minimal" or "negligible."
4. **The decline is first-class.** "Not now" is respected silently and reversibly; no nagging, no re-offers (Attendant Mode especially: once means once).
5. **The covenant posture, extended to the wallet:** nothing spends silently — same sentence shape as "an update never touches your files."
