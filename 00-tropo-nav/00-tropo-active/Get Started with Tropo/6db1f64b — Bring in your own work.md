You don't start Tropo from scratch — you start it with the work you already have. A strategy doc, a deck, a folder of notes sitting on your laptop right now: Tropo is built to take them in without locking them up. Your source stays yours, untouched, while a governed copy joins your graph.

## What to do

1. Find a document you care about — a `.docx`, `.pdf`, or `.pptx`.
2. Drop it into the **`04-external-work/`** folder at the root of your studio. (That folder's README explains the drop-zone.)
3. Ask your agent: *"Import the file I just dropped in 04-external-work."*
4. Ask: *"Show me what you just imported."* Your agent opens the new governed entry — now part of your vault, searchable and linkable alongside everything else.

## Why this matters

Most tools make you choose between your files and their system. Tropo does not. Your agent leaves the original exactly where it is, writes a hidden sidecar next to it, and creates a governed entry in your vault. The source stays private and in place; a governed projection joins the graph. That is the whole point of an operating system you own: it meets your work where it already lives.

## When you're done

You've dropped a real file into `04-external-work/`, imported it, and seen it appear as a governed entry in your vault. Next: edit the imported content with your agent, then export a branded copy back out. Mark this task done.
